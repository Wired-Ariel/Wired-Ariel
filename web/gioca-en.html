    Game

    In order: connect the Pico, then empty cartridge slot, cable, switch the GBA on and load the game; red screen ➜ insert the cartridge with the console on ➜ green ➜ play. Finally press Play once you're outdoors.

    Tools

    The Live map shows Hoenn with you and your friends live: it opens in a separate tab and takes positions from this page, so keep both open. Just watch is for those without a GBA at hand (or on Firefox, where WebUSB doesn't exist): you connect to the relay and see the others move, without sending anything — and without taking one of the room's 4 seats, so you can watch even when the game is full. If you're playing elsewhere (for example inside mGBA), enter that client's peer number in the Settings: on the map you'll show up as «you», in light blue.

    Playing inside mGBA? Pick the game version above and press Download the emulator script: you get a .lua file already set up with the room above — load it in mGBA (Tools → Scripting → Load script) and play, with nothing to edit by hand. It picks its own peer number. To change room later: download it again, or type stanza(4242) in the mGBA console (with your room number).

    The Cable Club (trades and battles) works from here too: both of you sit at the counter in the club room, and the rest starts by itself — the log says «passo il Pico in modo LINK». During the trade keep this tab in the foreground (in the background the browser slows down timers). The Pico must be connected: as a spectator, or inside mGBA, there is no club.

    Settings

    They must match your friends': same relay and same room. Nobody hosts anything: the relay lives on a server and everyone connects to it.

    First time on Windows? The Pico needs the WinUSB driver (installed once with Zadig: select device 2FE3:000A and press «Install driver»). Without the driver the browser sees the Pico but can't open it.
